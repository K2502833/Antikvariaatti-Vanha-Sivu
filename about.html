Our Story

Antikvariaatti Vanha Sivu is a second-hand bookshop in Ähtäri, Finland. We believe that every book deserves another reader and every story deserves to be remembered.

Our Collection

Our collection includes books from different authors, subjects and periods. We aim to help readers discover interesting books at affordable prices.
