# Website Proposal – Antikvariaatti Vanha Sivu

**Prepared for:** Erkki Lahti, Bookseller  
**Business:** Antikvariaatti Vanha Sivu, Ähtäri  
**Project:** Searchable Second-hand Book Catalogue

## 1. Goal and Must-Haves

The goal is to create a simple and user-friendly website for Antikvariaatti Vanha Sivu. Customers should be able to find second-hand books without having to visit the shop or search through an old spreadsheet.

The website should include:
- Book searches by title and author.
- Book information, including title, author, condition and price.
- A catalogue that displays books in smaller groups instead of showing everything at once.
- A wish form for customers who cannot find the book they want.
- Clear navigation and contact information.

## 2. Visitors

The website is designed for three main groups of visitors:

1. **Book lovers:** People looking for interesting second-hand books at affordable prices.
2. **Collectors:** People searching for specific authors, older editions or rare books.
3. **Local customers:** People in Ähtäri and nearby areas who want to discover the bookshop and contact the bookseller.

## 3. Content Plan

### Home – index.html
Introduce the bookshop, explain what it offers and welcome visitors. Include a link to explore the book catalogue.

### About – about.html
Introduce Antikvariaatti Vanha Sivu, its purpose and the value of buying and reusing second-hand books.

### Catalogue – listing.html
Present sample books with their titles, authors, conditions and prices. Include a planned search area and a way to view more books in smaller groups.

### Contact – contact.html
Provide contact information and a wish form where customers can request books that are not currently available.

## 4. Three Real Websites for Inspiration

**1. Antikvaari.fi – https://www.antikvaari.fi/**

This website brings together books from many Finnish antiquarian bookshops. Its search options and book categories make it easier to find specific books. I would use its clear catalogue and search approach as inspiration. However, our website will be simpler and focused on one local bookshop.

**2. Antikvariaatti Scriptorium – https://www.scriptorium.fi/**

This is a real second-hand bookshop with information about its shop and book catalogue. Its combination of shop information and catalogue content is useful. I would use this approach to introduce the business while making the catalogue easy to find.

**3. Lukulaari – https://www.lukulaari.fi/**

Lukulaari is a Finnish antiquarian bookshop with information about its shop, products and customer contact options. Its welcoming introduction and clear contact section are good examples. I would use these ideas to make our website friendly and easy to understand.

## 5. Tone

The website should feel welcoming, traditional, trustworthy and connected to the history of books.

Three key messages:

- Every book has a story worth discovering.
- Find old favourites and new reading experiences.
- If you cannot find a book, send us a wish.

The visual identity will use the client's selected colours:

- Deep plum: #301934
- Soft white: #f8f5fa
- Gold: #c9971a

The design should create an old-paper feeling while remaining simple and readable.

## 6. What We Need from the Client

To complete the website accurately, we will need:

- The existing book catalogue spreadsheet.
- Real book titles, authors, conditions and prices.
- Bookshop photographs or a logo.
- Confirmed contact details and opening hours.
- The email address that should receive customer book wishes.
- Information about the condition categories used by the shop.

Until this information is available, sample book information and clearly identified placeholders will be used. The wish form will initially show a confirmation message as a demonstration; connecting it to the client's email will be completed later.

## 7. Next Steps

1. Get approval for the proposed website structure and content.
2. Collect the existing book catalogue and other business information.
3. Create and publish the four main website pages.
4. Check navigation, accessibility and HTML validation.
5. Develop the catalogue search, gradual book loading and wish form in later project stages.
6. Connect the wish form to the client's email when the required technical details are available.
